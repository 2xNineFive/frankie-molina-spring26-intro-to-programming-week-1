About

I am 30 years young and I am from the Coachella Valley. I identify as a neurodivergent and latinx serial entrepreneur, artist, and activist. Specifically, I am clinically diagnosed as ADHD and Bipolar 2, and I also self-identify as autistic. I channel my neurodivergence through all my endeavors, and especially through my EDM project ALYVE and my digital artist project BPLR. I am in the process of building a workflow for my YouTube channel Frankie's World. The purpose of my channel is to share all the cool things I'm doing and building. I recently graduated from College of the Desert's & PaCE's Generative AI Foundations for Creativity certification program. I scored a 956 out of 1000 on my Certiport certification exam, and my professor said I had the most emotionally impactful capstone project out of the entire class. I've been simultaneously working on my MBA in Entrepreneurship at Oneday where I am building two businesses; one is an electrical company, and the other is a productivity app designed for neurodivergent entrepreneurs. My MBA mentor Eve recommended I pursue the Data Engineering course at Code The Dream, so I am also working on that. The goal is to utilize all these skills to fulfill my childhood dream of escaping poverty.But my goals do not end with personal enrichment. I also want to make structural changes in my communities at large to help others escape poverty too.
